About the Lotus

A Flower That Grows in Mud

The lotus is an aquatic plant that grows in muddy ponds and slow rivers. Its leaves and petals stay clean and dry because water rolls off them. This is known as the lotus effect. The flower closes at night and opens again in the morning.

Facts About the Lotus

Scientific name:
Nelumbo nucifera
Habitat:
Shallow ponds, lakes and slow rivers
Lifespan:
Seeds can stay alive for hundreds of years

Lotus seeds hold water, H2O, and a small amount of CO2 when they germinate.

A bloom can reach 30 cm across, and each bloom lasts about 3 days.

The 1st bloom of the season is usually in June.

Lotus seeds are rich in protein, and the cm measure above means centimetres.

Common misspellings: lotuss, lotas, lottus.

Lotus in Culture

The lotus flower is the symbol of purity, because it blooms beautifully even when it grows in muddy water.
