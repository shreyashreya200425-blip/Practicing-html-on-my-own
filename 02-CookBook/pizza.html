Classic Margherita Pizza

General Information

Prep Time
20 minutes
Cook Time
15 minutes
Serves
2 people

Ingredients

  • 250 g pizza flour
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 1 tbsp instant yeast
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 100 g fresh mozzarella
  • 100 g tomato passata
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • A pinch of black pepper

Method

  1. Combine the flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Add warm water and olive oil, then mix until a soft dough forms.
  2. Knead the dough for about five minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover it and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to its highest setting. Stretch the dough into a thin round and place it on a baking tray.
  4. Spread the tomato passata over the dough. Add mozzarella, basil leaves, and a little black pepper.
  5. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling. Remove from the oven and rest for two minutes.

Warning: the oven is very hot.

Bake at 250° C

Updated: Bake at 200° C
