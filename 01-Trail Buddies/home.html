About Us

Trail Buddies is a community built for people who believe the best way to explore the outdoors is together. From weekend escapes and beginner-friendly walks to challenging mountain routes, we create experiences that make getting outside feel approachable, social, and rewarding. Whether you're an experienced hiker or taking your first step onto the trail, there's a place for you here. We bring together curious explorers, nature lovers, and adventure seekers through thoughtfully planned hikes, local trail experiences, and a community that encourages everyone to discover more.

More Than a Trail. A Community.

Our philosophy is simple: Explore responsibly, challenge yourself, leave no trace, and never stop discovering.

Packages

Whether you're looking for a relaxed weekend outdoors or a more challenging mountain experience, Trail Buddies has an adventure for you.

Weekend Explorer

For casual hikers and first-time explorers.
A relaxed outdoor experience designed for people who want to disconnect from the routine and spend a day surrounded by nature.

  • Guided day hike
  • Scenic trail route
  • Small-group experience
  • Trail safety briefing
  • Refreshment break

Summit Seeker

For hikers ready for a challenge
Take your adventure further with a more demanding trail designed around elevation, endurance, and incredible views.

  • Guided mountain hike
  • Experienced trail leader
  • Route and weather briefing
  • First-aid support
  • Hydration and rest stops
  • Summit photography

Wild Escape

For the full outdoor experience
A multi-day experience designed for people who want to truly step away from the city and immerse themselves in the outdoors.

  • Multi-day guided adventure
  • Curated hiking routes
  • Campsite coordination
  • Group activities
  • Local experiences
  • Dedicated trip coordinator

Perfect for: Experienced hikers, groups, and anyone looking for a deeper outdoor escape.
