General Information

Prep Time
10 minutes
Cook Time
5 minutes
Serves
1 person

Ingredients

  • 2 vegetarian burger patties
  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 slices cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp BBQ sauce
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Method

  1. Divide the vegetarian burger mixture into two equal portions and shape each portion into a thick patty.
  2. Season both sides of the patties with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and cook the vegetarian patties for about four minutes on each side until golden and crisp.
  4. Toast the buns lightly, spread them with mayonnaise and BBQ sauce, then layer the lettuce, tomato, onion, and cheesy vegetarian patty inside.

    5. Warning: cook the vegetarian patties until they are hot and golden brown.

    As the chef says, Always rest your patty.

    This vegetarian burger is packed with delicious vegetables and plant-based goodness.

    Burger in Japanese: ハンバーガー ( Hanbaga )

    Serve hot
